A DAO vote that casts itself once the voters you trust agree

The owner picks a few voters they trust. When enough of them have voted the same way on a proposal, VoteMirror casts the owner's vote the same way, with no bot or server to run. Everything below is read from the two testnets by your browser.

  1. Base Sepolia Trusted voters vote on a Governor proposal, as they normally would.
  2. Reactive Lasna A reactive contract counts their votes and asks for a callback when the threshold is reached.
  3. Base Sepolia VoteMirror casts the vote or skips it if the owner paused it or opted out of that proposal.
